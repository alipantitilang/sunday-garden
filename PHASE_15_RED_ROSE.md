# Phase 15 — Red Rose Research

Status: complete

## Scope

This phase completes the `red-rose` flower record in `data/flowers.json`.

Red Rose is treated as a colour-based common category, not as a single species. The record therefore uses genus-level `Rosa` information and explicitly avoids assigning one species to Hyunwo's flower.

## Research layers

- Botanical / taxonomic identity
- Morphology
- Growth and life cycle
- Flowering and reproduction
- Distribution and habitat
- Ecology
- Conservation scope
- Historical and cultural records
- Documented symbolism
- Sunday Garden interpretation

## Editorial rule

Historical symbolism is presented as documented cultural usage, not as a universal botanical property. The Sunday Garden interpretation is clearly separated from those historical records.
