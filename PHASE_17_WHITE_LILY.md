# Phase 17 — White Lily Research

## Scope
Research and normalize the White Lily record without pretending the common category identifies a single species.

## Taxonomic decision
The canonical record remains `Lilium spp.`. Kew accepts the genus *Lilium*. *Lilium candidum* is used only as a documented species example because RHS and Kew both identify it with the common-name usage “white lily”; it is not asserted as the Gardener’s exact species.

## Completed layers
- taxonomy
- morphology
- distribution and habitat
- growth and reproduction
- ecology
- cultural history
- documented symbolism
- Sunday Garden interpretation
- source verification

## Editorial rule
Colour/category names do not automatically determine species. Any future identification of a specific Gardener’s White Lily must be supported by supplied imagery or other reliable identification evidence.
