# Phase 19 — Blue Lotus Research Audit

## Scope
Audit the existing `blue-lotus` flower record against the canonical research standard established in Phases 14–18.

## Audit results
- Taxonomy normalized to Kew's current accepted treatment: `Nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea (Savigny) Verdc.`
- `Nymphaea caerulea Savigny` retained as a synonym rather than presented as the current accepted name.
- The common name Blue Lotus is explicitly distinguished from true lotus in `Nelumbo`.
- Morphology and growth claims are tied to Kew / Flora Zambesiaca data and are not over-generalized where measurements vary by source.
- Distribution separates native range from introduced range.
- Cultural claims are restricted to documented Egyptian art, funerary, and ceremonial contexts.
- Symbolism is presented as historically contextual rather than universal.
- No unsupported pollinator or single ecological-role claim is made.
- No global conservation status is assigned without a directly applicable assessment for this accepted infraspecific taxon.
- A peer-reviewed 2023 chemistry/safety source was added to the research record. It is used as scientific context, not as a basis for medical claims.

## Editorial rule
The Gardener's personal interpretation remains separate from botanical and historical claims. The research record must not overwrite or invent a Gardener's story.
