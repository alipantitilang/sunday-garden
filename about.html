Where the garden began

02

It started on a Sunday.

Sunday Garden began as a small program inside Sunday Vibes, a community built around slowing down, spending time together, and making room for simple moments.

The original question was simple: “Kalau kamu bisa menjadi sebuah bunga, kamu ingin menjadi bunga apa?”

What came back was more interesting than a list of flowers. People chose flowers for memories, personality, places they had been, things they had survived, and hopes they were still carrying. The flower was never really the whole answer.

So this website became an extension of that little Sunday ritual — a place to keep those answers, let them grow, and discover the people behind them.

The idea

“The flower is the question;
the person is the answer.”

Different flowers. Different stories. One garden.

Our vision

A garden where people can be seen without having to explain everything.

We imagine Sunday Garden as a growing collection of small human stories — personal, imperfect, thoughtful, and different from one another.

Not every story needs to be long. Not every flower needs to carry a grand meaning. Sometimes choosing one is enough to start a conversation.

Our mission

Collect stories. Care for them. Let them bloom.

  • Give people a gentle prompt for self-expression.
  • Preserve each Gardener's story in their own voice.
  • Pair personal stories with thoughtful, sourced flower knowledge.
  • Build a garden that feels welcoming rather than competitive.
Garden Keeper

The Garden Keeper

Someone has to tend the little things.

Sunday Garden is curated and maintained by Alif, the Garden Keeper behind the project.

The role is less about deciding what a story should mean and more about taking care of the space: reviewing submissions, preserving each person's voice, checking flower information, and keeping the garden coherent as it grows.
